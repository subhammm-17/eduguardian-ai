Four tools, one workspace

Each tool is designed for a specific part of studying, from first understanding to exam revision.

01

Socratic tutoring

Switch to Socratic mode and the tutor asks guiding questions instead of giving the solution. A three-step hint ladder helps when you are stuck.

02

Chat with your syllabus

Upload lecture notes as PDF or text. Answers are drawn from your material, with citations to the exact file and page.

03

Flashcards and quizzes

Generate cards and quizzes from a topic or from your notes. Spaced repetition schedules each card for review at the right time.

04

Study roadmap

Enter your exam date and available hours. Receive a week-by-week plan that ends with dedicated revision days.

Answers you can trust

Language models can be confidently wrong, especially with algebra. EduGuardian AI checks every mathematical result it can with SymPy, an open-source computer algebra system, and labels the answers it has confirmed.

  1. 1

    The model drafts an answer

    The tutor writes an explanation or generates a quiz question.

  2. 2

    A solver checks the result

    Equations, derivatives and integrals are recomputed symbolically.

  3. 3

    Verified answers are labelled

    You see a Verified label only when the two results agree.
